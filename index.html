Background

Live-stream shopping was booming across Asia. We needed to understand why.

During COVID-19, shopping habits shifted dramatically. Live-stream commerce exploded across Asia, attracting major startup investment. Our mission was to understand the landscape, validate emerging opportunities, and determine whether live streaming could become a viable sales channel for independent fashion brands — or if a bigger opportunity was hiding underneath.

Starting hypothesis
Live streaming is an untapped sales channel for independent fashion brands that could rival established platforms like ASOS or Shopify.
Core questions
Do shoppers prefer indie brands over labels? Does live video influence purchase decisions? Who and what shapes buying behaviour?

Key findings from Phase 1
Growing appetite for indie brands — shoppers showed strong preference for independent, niche fashion over mainstream labels, driven by values alignment and uniqueness
Trust is the live-stream variable — live streaming can influence purchase decisions, but only when the host has genuine credibility and an established audience relationship
Target buyer profile — female shoppers 25–40, city-based, values-conscious, wants discovery and inspiration, frustrated by inability to find relevant independent products

Phase 2 — Journey Mapping

Tracing the full path from inspiration to purchase — and finding where it breaks

We explored how fashion stylists recommend outfits, how creators pick and present products, and how consumers actually discover and shop online. We mapped best and worst experiences, platform choices, and typical purchase categories — and identified exact friction points along the discovery-to-purchase journey.

Pain points uncovered along the journey
Unclear sizing & poor return policies — eroded trust before checkout; a primary driver of drop-off for independent brands
Lack of real product context — shoppers couldn't visualise items on themselves without seeing them styled in a real-life context
Fragmented collaboration — no single platform effectively connected brands, stylists, creators, and consumers into a coherent flow
Navigation complexity — existing platforms built for breadth, not curation; shoppers couldn't find niche or value-aligned products efficiently

Phase 4 — The Pivot

The data told a different story. We listened.

Across 150+ creator conversations, a consistent pattern emerged. Creators didn't need another live-stream sales channel. Their biggest pain was the fragmented, inefficient journey from creating content to getting paid — managing brand partnerships, tracking commissions, and navigating affiliate admin while trying to produce content.

The insight that changed everything
The real problem wasn't the sales channel —
it was the broken creator-to-payment pipeline.

We saw a bigger opportunity in the creator economy: build a tool that streamlines the entire promotion-to-payment workflow, so creators can focus on what they do best — creating — while monetising their influence with zero friction, zero negotiations, and zero admin overhead.

What we stopped building
Live streaming as the primary feature — it was a means to an end, not the real value creators needed
What we built instead
A style-to-shop creator tool: upload inspo → AI product matching → curated mini shop → automatic affiliate commission
